Do Google Ads and Meta Ads work the same way?

Not exactly. Google Ads often connects with active search intent, while Meta Ads can help you engage chosen audiences on Facebook and Instagram. The right mix depends on your goals.

No. Outcomes depend on the market, budgets, competition and execution. We focus on relevant planning, clear work and improvement.
